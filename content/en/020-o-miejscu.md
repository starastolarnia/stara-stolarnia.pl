Stara Stolarnia is a family-run wedding venue born from a love of craftsmanship and hospitality. Its original timber interiors feel warm and calm, with thoughtful details that need little extra decoration.

You will find us at the edge of the forest in Byków, just outside Wrocław. The greenery creates a beautiful setting for the celebration and photographs, while giving your guests the freedom, quiet and space to truly enjoy the day.

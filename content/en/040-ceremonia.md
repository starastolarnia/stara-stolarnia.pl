A ceremony among the birch trees has a rhythm of its own: leaves overhead, soft light and the people closest to you gathered truly close. The timber pavilion and woodland aisle create a naturally beautiful setting, without asking your guests to travel elsewhere.

After the congratulations, it is only a short walk back for the first toast and the start of the celebration.
